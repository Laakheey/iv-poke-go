Frequently Asked Questions: Pokémon GO PvP IVs & Stat Products

Why do Pokémon have 0 Attack IV in Great League and Ultra League?

In Pokémon GO, the CP formula weights Attack disproportionately compared to Defense and Stamina. Because Great and Ultra League have hard CP ceilings (1500 and 2500 CP), having a 0 Attack IV allows the Pokémon to reach a higher level, fitting significantly more total defense and stamina stats for a higher overall stat product.

What is Stat Product in GO Battle League?

Stat product is calculated by multiplying a Pokémon's effective Attack, Defense, and HP (Stat Product = Atk × Def × HP). The IV spread with the highest stat product under the CP cap is Rank #1 (100%).

What is Charge Move Priority (CMP)?

When both Pokémon fire a charge attack simultaneously, the Pokémon with the higher raw Attack stat fires first. Some players deliberately choose slight attack-weighted spreads to win CMP ties in mirror matches.
